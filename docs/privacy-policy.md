# Arkad Privacy Policy

_Effective November 22, 2026_

> We collect what's needed to run your account, your communities and your journal. We don't sell your personal information. You can export your data or delete your account at any time in Settings.

## 1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Arkad ("Arkad", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use the Arkad app and related services (the "Service").

## 2. Information you give us

- Account: your email address and password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; Arkad never sees or stores your password.
- Profile: your full name, username, bio and profile photo.
- Trading journal: the trades you enter (symbol, asset type, prices, quantity, dates, notes), your starting balance, deposits and withdrawals, and whether each trade is shown on your profile.
- Social activity: posts, images, comments, likes, bookmarks, follows, communities you create or join, channel messages, files you share, trade callouts, and reports and blocks you make.
- Support: what you send us when you email support.

## 3. Information collected automatically

The app includes Google Firebase Analytics, which records basic usage events (such as app opens, screens viewed and session length) and device information (such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and an app-instance identifier). We use this to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. We don't use it to show ads.

The app doesn't access your location, contacts or microphone. It uses your camera or photo library only when you choose to take or pick a photo to upload.

## 4. How we use information

- To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
- To run the Service: show your profile, feed, communities, chats, callouts, leaderboards and portfolio.
- To calculate your portfolio values, performance and leaderboard rankings.
- To moderate content, respond to reports, and prevent fraud, spam and abuse.
- To answer support requests and send important service messages.
- To understand usage and improve the app.
- To comply with the law and enforce our Terms.

## 5. What other users can see

Other signed-in users can see your profile (name, username, bio, photo, follower counts), your posts and comments, and trades you've chosen to show on your profile. Members of a community can see your messages, files and callouts in that community. Closed trades shown on your profile are used for leaderboards. Anything you post publicly can be seen, saved or shared by others.

## 6. How we share information

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share information only:

- With service providers who run the Service for us: Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage and analytics) and Finnhub (market news and quotes). For quotes, the app sends Finnhub only the ticker symbols of your open stock positions, never your account details.
- With Apple, if you make in-app purchases (when available).
- When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, Arkad or others.
- As part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

## 7. Where data is stored and how it's protected

Your data is stored by Google Firebase, which may process it in the United States and other countries. Data is encrypted in transit, and access is restricted to what's needed to run the Service. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 8. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account in Settings, your sign-in and profile are deleted straight away. Some content you created, such as trades, posts, comments, images and community messages, may remain until it is removed separately; you can delete individual trades and posts in the app, or email support@arkadtrader.com and we'll remove the rest. We may keep limited information when the law requires it or to resolve disputes and enforce our Terms.

## 9. Your choices and rights

- Access and export: Settings > Export Data creates CSV files of your trades and posts.
- Correct: edit your profile at any time.
- Control visibility: choose whether each trade is shown on your profile.
- Delete: delete posts and trades in the app, or your whole account in Settings > Delete Account.
- Block and report other users.

Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or the UK), you may have extra rights, such as asking what personal information we hold about you, asking us to correct or delete it, objecting to or restricting some uses, or complaining to a data protection authority. To make a request, email support@arkadtrader.com. We'll respond within the time the law requires and won't treat you differently for using your rights.

## 10. On your device

If you turn on Remember me, your email address is saved on your device so it's filled in next time. Your appearance setting (light or dark) is saved on your device. Data exports are created on your device and only leave it if you share them.

## 11. Children

Arkad is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has created an account, email support@arkadtrader.com and we'll delete it.

## 12. New features

If we add features that use new kinds of information, such as connecting a brokerage account or paying for a community, we'll update this policy and explain what's collected before you use them.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes we'll tell you in the app before they take effect. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.

## 14. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email support@arkadtrader.com.
